Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Rosalia Roqueira LLC, doing business as Emporion Pros (“Emporion Pros,” “we,” “us”), operates emporionpros.com and related services, including campaign listing pages, lead management tools, our AI assistant Aria, and our vendor marketplace. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company, role, property details, messages, tour or demo requests, and account details when you sign up or contact us.
  • Information from calls and texts: when you call or text us, including calls answered by our AI assistant, we collect your phone number, call recordings, transcripts, and the information you share during the conversation.
  • Information collected automatically: device and browser information, IP address, pages viewed and referring pages, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information from customers: agents, property managers and owners who use our platform may give us information about people who inquire about their properties.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve our services.
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule tours and demonstrations, and provide customer support.
  • To send messages you have requested or agreed to receive, including text messages (see “Text messages” below).
  • To connect property inquiries with the authorized property representative.
  • To protect against fraud and abuse and to comply with the law.

Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages from Emporion Pros, we use your mobile number only to send the messages described in our SMS Terms. We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with service providers who send messages on our behalf. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run our services, such as hosting, databases, telephone and texting, AI voice assistance, email, and customer relationship management (currently including Supabase, Netlify, Telnyx, Vapi, Google Workspace and Follow Up Boss). They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • With the property owner, manager or agent responsible for a property you inquire about, so they can respond to you.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of Emporion Pros, our users or others.
  • In connection with a merger, sale or transfer of our business, subject to this policy.

AI assistant and call recordings

Our AI assistant, Aria, identifies itself as an AI assistant. Calls with Aria may be recorded and transcribed so we can respond to your request and improve our service.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, to meet legal obligations, and to resolve disputes.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@emporionpros.com. You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email and stop texts by replying STOP. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, including New Jersey’s; we will respond to requests as those laws require.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No system is completely secure.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Fair housing

We do not use personal information to steer anyone toward or away from housing based on any protected characteristic.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new effective date on this page.
